# Troubleshooting and Outages

## First steps when the internet is down

1. Check the outage map at status.brightwave.example to see if there is a known outage in your area.
2. Restart your router: unplug it, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in. It takes about 3 minutes to come back online.
3. If the light still is not solid white, check that the cable from the wall is firmly connected to the router's WAN port.

## Router status lights

- Solid white: connected and working normally.
- Blinking white: starting up or installing an update. Do not unplug it.
- Blinking amber: no signal from the Brightwave network. Check the cable and the outage map.
- Solid red: hardware fault. Contact support for a free replacement router.

## Slow Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi speeds depend on distance and walls. For the best speed, place the router in a central, open spot, away from microwaves. Devices that support Wi-Fi 6 get the fastest speeds. Run a speed test at speed.brightwave.example with a cable connected to compare.

## Outage credits

If an outage in your area lasts more than 24 hours, you get a credit for each full day without service. The credit is added automatically to your next bill; you do not need to request it. Outages caused by your own equipment or wiring are not credited.
