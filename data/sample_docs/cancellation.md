# Cancelling Your Service

## How to cancel

You can cancel any time in the app under Account > Cancel service, or by calling support. Monthly customers do not need to give notice and have no contract. Service stays active until the end of the current billing cycle.

## Early termination fee

Only customers on a 12-month price lock pay an early termination fee. The fee is $150, reduced by $12.50 for each full month you have already completed.

## Returning equipment

Return your Brightwave router (and any mesh extenders) within 30 days of cancelling. Use the prepaid return label emailed to you, or drop the equipment at any partner shipping store. If the router is not returned within 30 days, a $150 unreturned equipment fee is charged.

## Final bill

Your final bill is sent within 10 days of cancellation and includes any remaining charges, fees, or credits.
