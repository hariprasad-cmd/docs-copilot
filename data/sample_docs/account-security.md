# Account and Security

## Resetting your password

Tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. We send a reset link to your account email that expires after 30 minutes.

## Two-step verification

Turn on two-step verification in the app under Account > Security. You can receive codes by text message or use an authenticator app.

## Support PIN

For your security, phone support asks for your 4-digit support PIN before discussing your account. Set or change your PIN in the app under Account > Security > Support PIN. Brightwave staff will never ask for your password.

## Adding an authorized user

The account holder can add up to two authorized users who may call support and make changes for the account. Authorized users cannot cancel service.
