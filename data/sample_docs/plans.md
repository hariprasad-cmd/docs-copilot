# Internet Plans

## Plan options

| Plan  | Download speed | Upload speed | Price per month |
|-------|----------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Basic | 100 Mbps       | 20 Mbps      | $40             |
| Plus  | 500 Mbps       | 50 Mbps      | $60             |
| Gig   | 1000 Mbps      | 100 Mbps     | $80             |

All plans have unlimited data with no data caps. Prices do not include taxes.

## Changing your plan

Upgrades can be made any time in the app and take effect within 1 hour. You pay the difference for the rest of the month, prorated by day.

Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, so you keep your current speed until then.

## 12-month price lock

New customers can choose a 12-month price lock. Your monthly price will not go up for 12 months. Price-lock customers who cancel early pay an early termination fee (see the Cancellation guide).

## Plans we do not offer

Brightwave only provides home internet. We do not offer cable TV, phone lines, or mobile plans.
