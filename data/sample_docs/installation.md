# Installation

## Self-install kit

The self-install kit is free. It ships within 2-3 business days of signing up and includes a Wi-Fi 6 router, a power adapter, and a step-by-step setup card. Most customers are online in about 15 minutes.

## Professional installation

A technician can install your service for a one-time fee of $99. The fee is waived for customers on the Gig plan. You choose a 4-hour arrival window when booking, and the technician will call 30 minutes before arriving. An adult (18+) must be home during the visit.

## Equipment

Every plan includes one Wi-Fi 6 router at no extra cost. A mesh extender for larger homes can be added for $10 per month. You may use your own router, but Brightwave support can only troubleshoot Brightwave equipment.

## Moving home

If you move, request a transfer in the app at least 7 days before your move date. Transfers are free if your new address is in the Brightwave service area.
