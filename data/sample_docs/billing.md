# Billing and Payments

Brightwave Internet is a fictional internet provider used as sample data for this project.

## When your bill is ready

Your bill is generated on the 1st of every month and is due on the 15th of the same month. You will get an email and an app notification as soon as the new bill is ready.

## Late fees

If your payment has not arrived by the 15th, there is a 5-day grace period. A late fee of $10 is added if the bill is still unpaid on the 20th. Service is paused if a bill stays unpaid for 45 days after the due date.

## Ways to pay

You can pay with a credit or debit card, a bank transfer (ACH), or PayPal, in the Brightwave app or at brightwave.example/pay. We do not accept cash or cheques, and payments cannot be made at retail stores.

## Autopay discount

Turn on Autopay and get a $5 discount on every monthly bill. Autopay charges your saved card or bank account on the due date. You can turn Autopay off at any time in the app under Billing > Autopay.

## Disputing a charge

If something on your bill looks wrong, open a billing ticket in the app within 60 days of the bill date. Disputed amounts are not charged late fees while the ticket is open.
